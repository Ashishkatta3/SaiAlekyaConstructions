Who we are

A local builder you can meet on site.

Sai Alekya Constructions is a family-run builder based in Tirupati, developing residential apartments under the Alekya Homes name. Two buildings handed over and two more rising across the city. We keep our projects small in number so every slab, wall and fitting gets attention — and so buyers deal directly with the people who build their home.

Every structure is an RCC frame designed to withstand wind and seismic loads, built with red-clay brick and quality blockwork, and handed over on the timeline we commit to.

Build quality

Specified down to the fittings.

What goes into an Alekya home — the same standard across every flat.

01

Structure

RCC framed structure designed to withstand wind and seismic loads, with red-clay brick or AAC/CLC block masonry.

02

Doors & windows

Teak-wood main door with quality hardware, teak-frame internal flush doors, UPVC/aluminium windows with grills and glass.

03

Plaster & paint

Double-coat cement plaster inside and out. Premium ACE exterior paint; two coats of emulsion over putty inside.

04

Flooring

2×2 vitrified tile flooring throughout; granite, marble or anti-skid ceramic in common areas.

05

Kitchen

Granite platform with steel sink, bore and municipal water provision, and an exhaust point. Glazed tile dado above the counter.

06

Toilets

EWC in the master with flush tank, hot & cold mixer with shower, IWC elsewhere — CP fittings of reputed make, designer glazed dado to door height.

07

Electrical

Concealed copper wiring, AC points in bedrooms, geyser points in bathrooms, 3-phase supply, individual meter and MCBs.

08

Lift & more

Six-passenger lift of reputed make, telephone/internet and cable-TV provision in bedroom and living room.

Note: Registration, water, sewerage, car parking, lift and electrical charges are borne by the purchaser. GST applicable as per government norms. Shelves, chajjas, lofts and arches are optional at extra cost. Full specification sheet available on request.
